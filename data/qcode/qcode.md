## Overview

QCode.cc gives developers one API key for Claude, GPT and Chinese models (GLM, Kimi, DeepSeek, Qwen). The request path selects the protocol: Anthropic Messages at `https://api.qcode.cc/api`, OpenAI Chat Completions and Responses at `https://api.qcode.cc/openai/v1`. Claude models are served only on the Anthropic endpoint and GPT models only on the OpenAI endpoint; the Chinese models work on both. Regional hosts are asia.qcode.cc and us.qcode.cc.

## Pricing

Paid. Usage is billed at official model prices (multiplier 1.0). Monthly plans with a daily USD quota start at $9 for 30 days; pay-as-you-go packs start at $4 and are valid for 7 days. No free tier.

## Payment

Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Alipay, WeChat Pay and cryptocurrency; invoices available. CNY and USD pricing.
